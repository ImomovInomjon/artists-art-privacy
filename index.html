Artists Art — rassomlar o'z asarlarini joylaydigan, xaridorlar esa ularni ko'rib, rassom bilan bevosita bog'lanadigan ilova. Ushbu siyosat ilova qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'ishi, ulardan qanday foydalanishi va ularni qanday o'chirish mumkinligini tushuntiradi.

1. Qanday ma'lumotlar yig'iladi

  • Hisob ma'lumotlari: ism, familiya, email, telefon raqami, Telegram username (ixtiyoriy), parol (parol faqat shifrlangan holda Firebase Authentication'da saqlanadi) va tanlangan rol (rassom yoki xaridor).
  • Joylashuv: siz kiritgan manzil. Agar "joylashuvni aniqlash" tugmasini bossangiz, telefonning taxminiy koordinatalari olinib, manzilga aylantiriladi. Joylashuv fonda kuzatilmaydi.
  • Rasmlar: profil rasmi va rassomlar yuklagan asar rasmlari, ularning nomi, janri, o'lchami, narxi va tavsifi.
  • Google orqali kirish: Google hisobingizdagi ism, email va profil rasmi.
  • Ilovadagi faollik: xaridor bosgan "yoqdi" (❤️) belgilari, rassom xaridorga bergan kontakt ruxsatlari va bildirishnomalar oxirgi marta qachon ko'rilgani.

Ilovada reklama, analitika yoki kuzatuv vositalari yo'q. Ma'lumotlar sotilmaydi va reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi.

2. Ma'lumotlardan foydalanish

  • Hisobga kirish va profilni ko'rsatish uchun.
  • Rassomning ismi, profil rasmi va asarlari ilovaga kirgan boshqa foydalanuvchilarga ko'rsatiladi.

  • Xaridorning telefon raqami, emaili va joylashuvi boshqa foydalanuvchilarga ko'rsatilmaydi.
  • Qidiruv va janr bo'yicha saralash uchun.

3. Ma'lumotlar qayerda saqlanadi

Hisob va profil ma'lumotlari Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) xizmatida, rasmlar esa Supabase Storage xizmatida saqlanadi. Ma'lumotlar internet orqali shifrlangan (HTTPS) ulanish bilan uzatiladi. Bu xizmatlar ma'lumotlarni O'zbekistondan tashqaridagi serverlarda saqlashi mumkin.

Artists Art — Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Artists Art lets artists publish their artworks and lets buyers browse them and contact the artist directly.

Data we collect

  • Account data: first and last name, email, phone number, optional Telegram username, password (stored only by Firebase Authentication), and role (artist or buyer).
  • Location: the address you enter, or your approximate location when you tap "detect location". There is no background tracking.
  • Photos: your profile photo and artwork images with their title, genre, size, price and description.
  • Google Sign-In: name, email and profile photo from your Google account.
  • In-app activity: likes a buyer gives, contact permissions an artist grants, and when you last viewed notifications.

There are no ads, analytics or trackers. We do not sell data.

How it is used and shared

Artist name, profile photo and artworks are visible to other signed-in users. An artist's phone number, Telegram and location are shown only to buyers the artist has approved. When a buyer likes an artwork, the artist sees the buyer's name and profile photo. Buyers' phone, email and location are not shown to other users. Data is stored with Google Firebase and Supabase and transmitted over HTTPS.

Account deletion

Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account; your profile and all artworks are removed immediately and permanently, together with related likes and contact permissions. If you cannot sign in, email inomjonimomov35@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Delete account"; requests are completed within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
